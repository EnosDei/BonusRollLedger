# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release.
- Records every bonus roll on every character: the boss, difficulty, your loot spec at the time, and what you got (items, gold, or nothing).
- One-click panel (`/brl` or the addon compartment button) with a per-boss summary ("The Coiled Altar (Heroic): Blood 1, Unholy 3") and the full roll list. Switch characters from the button at the top.
- Imports the bonus roll history Simulationcraft has already saved, for all your characters, on login.
